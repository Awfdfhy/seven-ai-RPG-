Seven RPG

A Korean-style RPG with deep character customization, tactical turn-based combat, dynamic quest systems, party management, and living world simulation. Built for mobile with FastAPI + Vanilla JS + Capacitor.

Game Systems

Every system is designed to work together, creating emergent gameplay and meaningful choices.

Character Customization

Create unique heroes with 8 classes, 6 backgrounds, detailed appearance options, and stat allocation. Every choice affects gameplay.

Tactical Combat

Turn-based combat with positioning (frontline/backline), abilities with cooldowns, mana management, status effects, and difficulty scaling.

Quest Journal

Dynamic quest chains with branching objectives, multiple reward types, NPC dialogue integration, and world-state persistence.

Party Management

Recruit companions with distinct archetypes (Tank, DPS, Support, Controller), stances, AI behaviors, and relationship systems.

Title & Reputation

Earn titles through achievements that affect NPC reactions, unlock dialogue options, and provide permanent stat bonuses.

Living World Simulation

Day/night cycles, seasonal changes, ecosystem dynamics, weather systems, and NPC schedules that continue while you play.

Skill Check System

D20-based skill resolution with attribute modifiers, proficiency, advantage/disadvantage, and critical success/failure mechanics.

Accessibility First

Colorblind modes (4 types), text scaling (4 levels), high contrast, simplified controls, and full keyboard navigation.

Mod Support

Full modding framework with JSON data packs, asset overrides, custom quests, items, and classes. Steam Workshop ready architecture.

Analytics & Telemetry

Privacy-respecting gameplay analytics, progression tracking, economy monitoring, and session insights for balance tuning.

Endgame Content

New Game+, raids, challenge modes, special quests, and bounty systems with scaling difficulty and exclusive rewards.

VR/AR Ready

Capacitor-based architecture with WebXR support for future VR/AR gameplay modes and spatial UI experiments.
