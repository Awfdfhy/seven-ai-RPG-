# Seven RPG Canon Integrity
Before RPG changes, read `.seven-team/cohesion/RPG_V2_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md` and relevant canon/world tests.
- Keep immutable canon, mutable world state, character knowledge, relationships, player decisions, and derived summaries distinct.
- A character may use only information established and available to that character at the current point in the story.
- Do not narrate the player's character's unchosen dialogue, thoughts, decisions, or actions.
- Choices must have traceable downstream consequences; avoid cosmetic-only branching where the contract requires persistence.
- Maintain continuity across at least 20 turns and across exit/reopen where the contract requires it.
- Add deterministic tests for knowledge leaks, contradictions, choice consequences and save/reload continuity.
- Treat reports as evidence pointers, not proof that the current code passes.
